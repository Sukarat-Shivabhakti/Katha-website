About कथा.

A digital canvas for thoughts, stories, and the journey of learning to build for the web.

The Mission

I built कथा. (Katha) because I wanted a space that felt entirely my own. In a world of noisy social media feeds and endless scrolling, I wanted to create a quiet, minimalist corner of the internet where stories and ideas take center stage.

This platform serves as both a personal digital diary and a technical playground. It is a live demonstration of my journey in full-stack development, built from scratch using Django, Python, and Tailwind CSS.

The Creator

Hello! My name is Sukarat Shivabhakti. I am a passionate developer and storyteller. When I am not writing code or debugging Django errors, you can usually find me playing video games, exploring new tech, or watching great movies.

This project is a reflection of my dedication to learning and building things that are not only functional but also beautifully designed.
